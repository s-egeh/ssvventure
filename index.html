MR. FOSTER KOSI EGEH

CEO & FOUNDER

Leadership

The founder and Managing Proprietor of SSV Ventures, has over 22 years of working experience in the Banking Industry in Ghana and other Accounting roles.

He worked at The Royal Bank (GH) Ltd, Sahel Sahara Bank Ltd, CAL Bank Ltd, and KaKum Rural Bank Ltd.

He holds a Certificate in Petroleum, Oil & Gas Management from GIMPA, an MBA (Finance) from GIMPA, and a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Cape Coast.

He is responsible for all day-to-day management of the business and strategy, bringing visionary leadership and extensive experience in international trade and strategic business development.

MRS. REGINA AFUA EGEH

HEAD OF HR, ADMIN AND OPERATIONS

HR & Operations

As the Co-Founder and Head of HR, Admin and Operations, Mrs. Egeh has over 25 years of exceptional experience as an educator.

She leads the Venture's Human Resources, administration, and day-to-day operations.

Her expertise is crucial for people management, administrative excellence, and smooth operations across the organization.
